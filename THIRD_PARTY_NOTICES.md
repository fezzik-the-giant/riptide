## Theme palettes

The built-in themes in `assets/themes/` adapt these palettes. The four
Catppuccin flavours use the published values unchanged. The others are mapped
onto Catppuccin's colour roles, with in-between tones filled in where the
original palette has no matching colour. Cyberpunk is Riptide's own.
